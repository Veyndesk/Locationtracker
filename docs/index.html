What this can and cannot tell you
  1. The result is the location an ISP registered for a whole IP range, not a device. Typical error is 5–50 km.
  2. Mobile carrier addresses resolve to the carrier's gateway, which can be hundreds of kilometres from the phone.
  3. A VPN, proxy or CDN returns the exit node, not the person behind it.
  4. No IP lookup returns a street address. Only the GPS on the other tab gets close, and only with the user's consent.

How to find your location

Three steps, no app or account needed. Works on Android, iPhone, Windows and Mac.

1Press “Get my location”

Your browser asks for permission once. Allow it so the page can read your device's GPS.

2Wait a few seconds

The first reading is often rough. The tool keeps listening and keeps the most accurate fix, usually within 5–20 m outdoors.

3Copy or share

Copy your latitude and longitude, copy the full reading as JSON, or open the spot in Google Maps.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find my exact location?

Press “Get my location” and allow location access. The tool listens to your device's GPS for up to 20 seconds and keeps the most accurate reading, usually within 5–20 metres outdoors.

How do I get my latitude and longitude?

Once your location is found, your latitude and longitude are shown in decimal degrees. Use “Copy coordinates” to copy them, or open them directly in Google Maps.

How accurate is IP address location?

IP location shows where an internet provider registered a range of addresses, not where a device is. It is usually accurate to the city, with typical errors of 5–50 km, and can be much further off for mobile networks and VPNs.

Is my location stored or shared?

No. Everything runs in your browser. There is no server and nothing is saved. Your browser only contacts the map tile server and the IP lookup providers.

Why is my location wrong or not working?

Make sure location services are on for your device and browser, allow access for this site, and try near a window or outdoors. Desktop computers without GPS rely on Wi-Fi, which is less precise.
